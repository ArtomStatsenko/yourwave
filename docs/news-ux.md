# News and catch-up

The **Sources** tab groups all news switches, their last source-check status, and Reddit/Telegram channel lists. **Technology news · Horizon** identifies the broad Hacker News/RSS selection that was previously labelled only Horizon. **Manage** expands the saved subreddits or public channels beside their switch; drafts and open lists survive polling. Disabling a source preserves its saved list and excludes it from new automatic and catch-up selection without restarting. Already queued or playing material retains the existing queue policy. Other integrations remain below the news sources, and non-source experiments stay in **Experiments**.

Clock editions at :00 and :30 may start after the current song, up to 15 minutes late. A newer due edition supersedes older overdue occurrences, including after sleep or a manual move. Expired windows do not become a queue of missed editions. Weather and non-news inserts retain their five-minute window; timed reminders retain their own deadlines.

GitHub Trending discovers candidates, but popularity alone is not a news event. Its news items require a published stable release, dated by `published_at`; draft releases and prereleases are excluded. New projects require a recent repository `created_at`. Each release has its own identity, so a previously covered project may return with a new version. Legacy unaired GitHub discoveries without event dates are skipped at station startup; their source records and history are retained. This is bounded discovery from the existing Trending and new-project sources, not monitoring every GitHub repository.

The toolbar's **More news** action prepares a manual roundup of up to six saved, unplayed stories from the last 48 hours. It does not fetch the entire internet's missed news or revive the normal queue. It appears as an ordinary **Host edition**, including in the plan. Song announcements immediately following an edition are folded into the same history entry, with the original event IDs retained for the current-on-air marker; a song or session boundary prevents folding. Expanding the entry shows the text and inline sources without separate announcement or category headings. Public Reddit/Telegram posts collected within 48 hours may remain in the archive after normal freshness expires. One editorial request sees all selected stories and writes a connected account, grouping related material without inventing relationships. Dates are spoken naturally once per group (today, yesterday, or day and month), with exact source dates retained in metadata. Horizon's date remains a selection date because that adapter does not provide the underlying article's publication time. Source links and source text remain inspectable in history. Star counts and repeated source introductions give way to each tool's concrete purpose. Whole source texts are selected within an 18,000-character context budget; individual texts over 8,000 characters are skipped rather than clipped through a possible qualification.

Catch-up uses the existing editorial, TTS, package, speech-budget and song-boundary paths. Repeated clicks reuse pending work. Selected originals are reserved while the roundup is pending; preparation alone never marks them played. Completion records coverage and skips their remaining automatic packages. Skipping a roundup releases its originals without claiming completion. Starting the station remains a separate action.

Checks use isolated data directories. Production restart and human listening are separate deployment and quality checks.
